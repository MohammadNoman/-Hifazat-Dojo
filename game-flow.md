# Hifazat Dojo - Game Flow

## 1. Hero page

- Show the title: **Hifazat Dojo: AI-Powered Multi-Level Scam aur Phishing Awareness Game**.
- Show a short promise: **"Scam pehchano, apni hifazat karo, aur Community Guardian bano."**
- Include **Start Game**, **How to Play**, and **For Teachers** buttons.
- Display the offline notice: all examples are fictional; never enter real passwords, verification codes, identity details, or payment details.

## 2. Language selection

- Let users choose **Urdu**, **English**, or **Sindhi**.
- Keep the selected language throughout the game. The first version can show Urdu and English content, with a Sindhi translation pack added later.
- Offer an optional student-recorded audio button for each scenario. Do not use AI-generated voices in the exhibition video.

## 3. Level selection

- Show four locked/unlocked Dojo levels.
  1. **Aasaan Hifazat** - Easy - Class 6-7
  2. **Hoshiyar Hifazat** - Medium - Class 8-9
  3. **Expert Hifazat** - Hard - Class 10
  4. **Community Guardian** - Very Hard - Teachers, parents, and adults
- Each level contains 10 questions. A user needs 7 correct answers to unlock the next level.
- Show a small safety theme for every level: password, phishing, money scams, and community reporting.

## 4. Quiz screen

- Display progress, for example: **Question 3 of 10**.
- Show the fictional scenario in a message, call, email, or QR-card layout.
- Ask: **"Kya yeh Safe hai ya Scam?"**
- Give only two large buttons: **Safe** and **Scam**.
- After an answer, show the hint, Urdu explanation, English explanation, and a practical next action.
- Award 10 points for a correct first answer. Award a smaller learning point after an incorrect answer and explanation.
- Never collect a user's real personal, account, payment, or identity data.

## 5. Level result

- Show score out of 100, correct answers, and three safety habits learned.
- At 70% or higher: show **Level Cleared** and unlock the next level.
- Below 70%: offer **Try Again** and show the questions that need review.
- Suggested badges: **Password Protector**, **Phish Finder**, **Scam Stopper**, and **Community Guardian**.

## 6. Certificate

- After completing a level, generate an offline printable certificate with learner name, completed level, score, date, and badge.
- Keep the certificate local to the device unless the teacher exports it.
- After all four levels, award **Hifazat Dojo Community Guardian**.

## 7. Teacher dashboard

- Protect dashboard access with a local teacher passphrase; do not use student passwords.
- Show only anonymous or consented class data: number of learners, level completion, average score, common mistake categories, and pre/post quiz results.
- Export a local CSV or PDF summary for the STREAM project evidence.
- Include a **Community Safety Action** section: print family safety cards, discuss common scam patterns, and list verified official reporting channels without hard-coding personal data.
